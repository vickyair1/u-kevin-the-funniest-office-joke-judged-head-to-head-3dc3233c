# The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head

> "The fire drill went off during my performance review, and for the first time all year, everyone in the office agreed on the exit strategy."

A deterministic Python program that writes exactly one office joke (138 characters, one line) to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout.

Why this line: a real joke with a setup (fire drill during a performance review) and a turn (the whole office finally agreeing on the "exit strategy" — the fire exit, and quitting). It turns on two things anyone who has worked in an office knows — the fire drill and the annual review — and the punchline lands last.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
